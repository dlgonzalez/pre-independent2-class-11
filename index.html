Unit 5 · Final project

Back to our environmental project

The games have activated the language you need. Now use it to explain the project you researched.

You are not giving a formal presentation. You are having a conversation: both partners ask questions, explain and react.

15-minute preparation checklist

  1. Choose the environmental project you researched.
  2. Identify the problem and where it happens.
  3. Find out what materials or resources it uses.
  4. Understand the process: how does it work?
  5. Identify its environmental and social benefits.
  6. Think about whether it could work in Colombia.
  7. Prepare a natural conversation, not a speech to read.

Grammar · First look

Past Perfect — Which happened first?

First, notice how English shows that one past event happened before another past event or time.

“By the end of the 19th century, the Industrial Revolution had changed the world forever.”

Think about it

  1. Which happened first: the Industrial Revolution changing the world or the end of the 19th century?
  2. Which verb phrase shows the earlier event?
  3. What form of the verb follows had?
Discovery rule: Past Perfect = had + past participle. It describes an action that happened before another action or reference point in the past. The later event is often in the Simple Past.
